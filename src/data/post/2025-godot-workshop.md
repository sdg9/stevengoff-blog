The early-2025 **NodeBuster** history is less about another attack and more about the project around the game.

January includes a change toward a more component-based, modular file structure. March upgrades the project to Godot 4.4, adds the engine-generated UID files, and revisits documentation.

The same period also records design documents created with ChatGPT's help and changes to Copilot instructions.

## More context for the next change

Those instruction and design files are an early marker of AI entering the workflow around this particular project. The commits establish that I was preparing documentation and guidance; they don't measure how much faster the work became.

That distinction matters because the useful question isn't simply whether AI was present. It is whether the next person—or tool—making a change can understand the shape of the project.

Modular organization, an explicit engine version, and written design notes all help with that problem. They can also expose decisions that were previously implicit in scattered files.

I like keeping this as a separate chapter from the [late-2024 gameplay work](/2024-nodebuster). A prototype needs periods of adding behavior and periods of making the accumulated behavior easier to work on. Early 2025 shows the latter, before the much larger burst of projects later that year.
